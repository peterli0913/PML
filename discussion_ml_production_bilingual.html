03 · Problem P1

Batch quality-attribute prediction before the analytical result arrives

在分析结果出来之前预测批次质量属性
P1 Predict CQAs from process trajectory 由工艺轨迹预测关键质量属性
Motivation业务动因
Analytical results arrive days after the batch finishes. By then the only options are rework, downgrade or disposal. A usable prediction at an intermediate step would allow intervention while the batch is still live.
检测结果在批次结束后数天才出，届时只能返工、降级或报废。若能在中间步骤给出可用预测，就还有干预机会。
Formulation问题形式
Per batch i of project p, predict CQA vector y (assay, named impurities, water content, particle attributes) from process trajectory X(t), raw-material certificate attributes z, equipment identity and in-process control results.
ŷip = f( Xip(t ≤ τ), zip, eip )   plus an interval, not a point
Two framings are possible: regression on CQA values, or direct estimation of P(out of specification). I lean to regression with a calibrated interval, because the specification limit is known and can be applied afterwards.
两种形式：直接回归 CQA 值，或估计超标概率。我倾向回归加可标定区间——规格限已知，可事后套用。
Data数据
  • Process trajectories from DCS: temperature, pressure, agitation, addition rate, jacket duty — seconds to minutes resolution
  • Electronic batch records: step timings, actual charges, operator actions, in-process results
  • Raw-material certificates: assay and impurity profile of incoming materials
  • Laboratory results as labels, at batch granularity
DCS 工艺轨迹（秒至分钟级）、电子批记录（步骤时间、实际投料、操作、中控结果）、 原料 COA（含量与杂质谱）、实验室结果作为批次级标签。
Difficulties主要困难
  • Small n. Tens of batches per project, so per-project models are weak and pooling needs a transferable representation
  • Label granularity mismatch. Features are time series; labels are one value per batch
  • Trajectory misalignment. Variable step durations; batches are not time-comparable without warping or phase segmentation
  • Extreme imbalance. Nearly all historical batches pass specification
  • Cold start. A new project has no batches at all when the first prediction is wanted
小样本；特征是时序而标签是批次级；轨迹时长不一需对齐或分段；历史批次几乎全部合格； 新项目首批时无任何历史。
Candidates候选方法
Phase-based trajectory summarisation (per unit operation: duration, integral, extremum, ramp rate, deviation from setpoint) feeding regularised linear models or gradient boosting; hierarchical Bayesian models with partial pooling across projects; Gaussian processes where calibrated uncertainty matters more than accuracy; conformal prediction as a distribution-free wrapper to obtain guaranteed coverage.
按单元操作做轨迹特征汇总（时长、积分、极值、升温速率、偏离设定点程度）后接正则化回归或梯度提升； 跨项目部分汇聚的分层贝叶斯；需要可标定不确定度时用高斯过程；用共形预测获得无分布假设的覆盖保证。
QUESTION FOR YOU 请你判断
At roughly 30–50 batches per project, is cross-project pooling with a chemistry-aware representation actually better than a simple per-project model — or does the heterogeneity between molecules destroy the benefit?
在每项目约 30–50 批的量级下，用"含化学信息的表示"做跨项目汇聚，是否真的优于简单的单项目模型？ 还是分子间异质性会把好处抵消掉？

04 · Problem P2

Finding a robust sub-region inside an already-approved parameter range

在已批准的参数范围内寻找更稳健的子区间
P2 Data-driven robust operating region 数据驱动的稳健操作区间
Motivation业务动因
Registered ranges are wide because they were justified conservatively. Experienced operators know that within that range some settings run more smoothly than others, but this is tacit. We want to state it explicitly and monitor against it.
注册范围通常偏宽（当初论证保守）。有经验的操作者知道范围内某些设定更稳，但属隐性知识。 我们希望把它显式化并用于监控。
Formulation问题形式
Estimate the set of critical process parameter settings for which the probability that all quality attributes stay within specification exceeds a threshold, constrained to lie inside the approved range.
R = { u ∈ Uapproved : P( y(u) ∈ S ) ≥ 1 − α }
This is essentially a data-driven version of the design space concept, but estimated from routine manufacturing records rather than designed experiments.
即估计"使全部质量属性合格概率不低于阈值"的参数集合，且必须落在已批准范围内。 本质上是数据驱动的设计空间，但用常规生产记录而非设计实验来估计。
Difficulties主要困难
  • One-class data. Almost all historical batches are in specification, so there is little signal about where the boundary is
  • Almost no input excitation. Operators hold setpoints tightly, so the achieved parameter cloud is a small blob, not a spread
  • Confounding. Parameter drift correlates with equipment, campaign, season and operator crew
  • Causality. A region that correlates with good outcomes is not necessarily the cause of them
  • Deliberate perturbation is expensive and generally not acceptable on commercial batches
近似单类数据、输入几乎无激励、参数漂移与设备/批次活动/季节/班组混杂、相关不等于因果、 在商业批上刻意扰动成本高且通常不被接受。
Candidates候选方法
Quantile regression on the margin to specification rather than on the attribute itself; density-ratio or one-class approaches to describe the achieved region; Bayesian estimation of the probability surface with development-scale designed experiments used as a prior; Bayesian optimisation or active learning only where experimentation is permitted, which in practice means development and pilot scale rather than commercial.
对"距规格的余量"做分位数回归而非直接回归属性；用密度比或单类方法刻画已实现区域； 以研发阶段的设计实验作为先验，对合格概率曲面做贝叶斯估计； 仅在允许实验的环节（研发与中试，而非商业批）使用贝叶斯优化或主动学习。
QUESTION FOR YOU 请你判断
This is the problem where I think your side has the advantage: could development-scale designed experiments be structured so that they serve as an informative prior for the commercial-scale probability surface — rather than being used only to justify the registered range?
这题我认为你们研发侧更有优势：能否把研发阶段的设计实验组织成"可作为商业规模合格概率曲面先验"的形式， 而不是只用于论证注册范围？

05 · Problem P3

Predicting what changes on scale-up — the hybrid modelling case

预测放大过程中会发生什么变化——混合建模的典型场景
P3 Scale-up outcome and failure-mode prediction 放大结果与失效模式预测
Motivation业务动因
Recurring pattern: a route performs well at laboratory or pilot scale, then at commercial scale an unexpected impurity appears, or yield drops in a specific step. The knowledge of why tends to sit with a few senior engineers and is not reliably reused across projects.
反复出现的情形：路线在实验室或中试表现良好，放大后出现预期外杂质或某步收率下降。 原因认知集中在少数资深工程师身上，难以跨项目稳定复用。
Formulation问题形式
Rather than predicting absolute outcomes, predict the change from small to large scale as a function of scale-dependent physical quantities and equipment capability.
Δy = g( reaction class, rate-limiting regime, mixing time, heat-removal capacity, addition rate per volume, surface-to-volume ratio, ... )
Alternatively as classification over known failure modes: impurity formation from local hot spots, mixing-limited selectivity loss, extended hold time effects, solids handling and filtration behaviour.
不预测绝对结果，而是把"从小试到大规模的变化量"建模为与尺度相关的物理量及设备能力的函数； 或对已知失效模式做分类：局部过热生成杂质、混合受限导致选择性下降、保持时间延长的影响、固体处理与过滤行为。
Difficulties主要困难
  • Physics dominates and the relevant regime differs by chemistry, so a single data-driven model is unlikely to generalise
  • One or two scale-up events per project — the effective sample size is the number of projects, not batches
  • Historical records are unstructured — reports and review minutes rather than tabulated outcomes
  • Client isolation bites hardest here, because the useful signal is exactly the process detail we cannot pool
物理机理主导且主控机制随化学体系而变；每项目仅一两次放大事件，有效样本量是项目数而非批次数； 历史记录多为报告与评审纪要等非结构化形式；客户隔离在此题影响最大，因为有用信号恰是不可合并的工艺细节。
Candidates候选方法
Compute mechanistic quantities first — mixing time, Damköhler-type ratios, heat-removal margin, characteristic times — and use those as features so that learning happens in a physically meaningful, dimensionless space. Then either learn a correction to the mechanistic prediction, or learn failure-mode risk at the level of reaction class and limiting regime, which is the abstraction level that can legitimately be shared across clients.
先算机理量（混合时间、Damköhler 类比值、移热余量、特征时间），以其作为特征， 使学习发生在有物理意义的无量纲空间；再选择"对机理预测学残差修正"， 或在"反应类型 + 主控机制"层面学习失效风险——这一抽象层级是可以合法跨客户共享的。
QUESTION FOR YOU 请你判断
Is a cross-project model at the level of reaction class and rate-limiting regime realistic, or is scale-up fundamentally case-by-case such that the honest deliverable is a well-structured knowledge base with mechanistic calculators, and not a predictive model at all?
在"反应类型 + 主控机制"层面建跨项目模型现实吗？还是放大本质上只能逐案处理， 诚实的交付物应该是"结构化知识库 + 机理计算工具"，而根本不是预测模型？

06 · Problem P4

Flagging which step of an upcoming batch carries elevated risk

识别即将执行的批次中哪一步风险偏高
P4 Deviation and process-failure risk偏差与过程失效风险
Motivation业务动因
Deviations are currently investigated after the fact. If we could indicate before a shift which steps are most likely to go wrong, the response is cheap and non-invasive: add a second check, slow the addition, have a senior engineer present, increase sampling.
目前偏差都是事后调查。若能在班前指出哪些步骤最可能出问题， 应对手段成本很低且非侵入：增加复核、放慢加料、安排资深工程师在场、加严取样。
Formulation问题形式
Two variants. Offline: step-level risk scoring before execution from static context — step type, crew experience, equipment status, campaign position, historical deviation rate for that step. Online: anomaly detection on the running trajectory against the envelope of historically successful batches.
两种形式。离线：执行前按静态上下文对步骤打风险分（步骤类型、班组经验、设备状态、 在批次活动中的位置、该步历史偏差率）。在线：将运行中的轨迹与历史成功批次的包络做异常检测。
Difficulties主要困难
  • Rare events with inconsistent labelling granularity in deviation records
  • Mixed causes — human error, equipment condition and process sensitivity are entangled
  • Sensitivity of use. Anything that looks like scoring individual staff is unacceptable, so features must stay at role or process level
  • Self-defeating success. If the warning works, the event does not occur, which complicates evaluation
稀有事件且偏差记录的标注粒度不一致；人为、设备与工艺敏感性原因混杂； 任何看起来像给个人打分的做法都不可接受，特征须停留在岗位或过程层面； 预警成功则事件不发生，评估口径需特别设计。
Candidates候选方法
Multivariate statistical process control on batch trajectories — principal component or partial least squares models of the successful-batch envelope, with dynamic time warping for alignment. This is the established pharmaceutical approach, works at modest sample size, and is far easier to explain to quality assurance than a modern black-box alternative.
对批次轨迹做多变量统计过程控制——用主成分或偏最小二乘刻画成功批包络，配合动态时间规整对齐。 这是制药行业成熟做法，在样本量有限时可用，且比现代黑箱方法更容易向质量部门解释。
QUESTION FOR YOU 请你判断
Honest question rather than a rhetorical one: at our sample sizes, does modern machine learning actually outperform well-implemented partial least squares and multivariate control charts on this class of problem — and if not, should we deliberately start there?
这是真问题不是设问：在我们的样本量下，现代机器学习在这类问题上真的优于实现良好的 偏最小二乘与多变量控制图吗？如果不是，我们是否应该刻意从后者开始？

08 · Constraints

Five constraints that should drive the method choice

应当主导方法选择的五项约束

1 · Small effective sample size

Tens of batches per project, and for scale-up questions the effective unit is the project, not the batch. This argues for strong priors, partial pooling and parsimonious models over high-capacity ones.
每项目数十批；放大类问题的有效单位是项目而非批次。 这支持使用强先验、部分汇聚与低复杂度模型，而非高容量模型。

2 · Cold start on every new molecule

Prediction is most valuable exactly when we have no history for that molecule. Any useful approach must transfer from past projects through a representation that is chemically meaningful rather than project-specific.
预测最有价值的时点恰恰是该分子尚无历史数据时。 任何可用方法都必须通过"有化学含义而非项目专属"的表示，从历史项目迁移过来。

3 · No pooling across clients

We may learn generalisable relationships but must not transfer specific process parameters between clients. The practical question is which abstraction level is both legitimate to share and still informative — reaction class and limiting regime look like candidates.
可以学习可泛化的规律，但不得在客户之间迁移具体工艺参数。 实际问题是"哪一抽象层级既可合法共享又仍然有信息量"——反应类型与主控机制看起来是候选。

4 · Advisory-only deployment

Under good manufacturing practice the model cannot change setpoints or release material. Output is a draft, a prompt or a flag; a person decides. Consequences: calibrated uncertainty matters more than point accuracy, and inputs, model version and the human decision must all be recorded.
GMP 下模型不能修改设定值或放行物料，输出只能是草稿、提示或标记，由人决策。 由此：可标定的不确定度比点预测精度更重要；输入、模型版本与人工裁定都必须留痕。

5 · Mechanistic knowledge is available — and I think under-used

Unlike many industrial settings we are not starting from a black box. Kinetics, thermodynamics, transport correlations and process simulation are all in routine use. That makes hybrid formulations natural: compute physically meaningful quantities, learn in that space, or learn a correction to a mechanistic prediction. My working assumption is that this is the single largest advantage we have, and the place where your combination of chemistry and machine learning is most useful to us.
与许多工业场景不同，我们不是从黑箱开始——动力学、热力学、传递关联式与流程模拟都在常规使用。 这使混合建模成为自然选择：先算出有物理意义的量并在该空间中学习，或对机理预测学习修正项。 我的工作假设是：这是我们最大的优势，也是你"化学 + 机器学习"的组合对我们最有价值的地方。
